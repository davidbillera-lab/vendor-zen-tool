===============================================================
  EstateSales Upload Agent -- Colleague Setup Guide
===============================================================

WHAT THIS DOES
--------------
Takes a Denver Online Auctions (DOA) auction that is already live
and copies it into an EstateSales.net sale you are building: it
reads the public DOA auction page (one photo + title per lot),
uploads the photos to your sale's Pictures step, and types each
lot's title into that picture's Description. Then it STOPS. You
click "Save and Continue" and finish the sale yourself.

It runs on YOUR computer, in YOUR Chrome, with YOUR logins.
Nothing is shared with anyone.


YOU NEED
--------
  - A Windows PC and an internet connection
  - Node.js (free) -- https://nodejs.org  (pick the LTS version)
  - Google Chrome installed (the real one, not Edge)
  - Your estatesales.net login and your DOA login
  - The DOA auction already published (visible to the public)
  - A NEW estatesales.net sale started, sitting on the Pictures
    step of the wizard, with NO pictures in it yet


ONE-TIME SETUP  (about 10 minutes)
----------------------------------
  1. Unzip the folder David sent you somewhere permanent,
     e.g.  C:\estatesales-agent
  2. Double-click  SETUP.bat  and let it finish.
  3. When Notepad opens your login file, fill in:
       ESTATESALES_EMAIL=  and  ESTATESALES_PASSWORD=
       DOA_EMAIL=          and  DOA_PASSWORD=
     Leave every other line alone. Save, close Notepad.
  4. Double-click  SIGN-IN-ONCE.bat . A Chrome window opens on
     the estatesales.net sign-in page. Sign in yourself, tick
     "Remember Me" if it isn't ticked. The window closes on its
     own once you're in. That's the only time you sign in by hand.

If step 4 says your password was wrong and you're sure it isn't:
that's estatesales.net's bot check being suspicious of a brand-new
browser profile. Close it, wait a minute, run SIGN-IN-ONCE.bat again.


EVERY SALE
----------
  1. On estatesales.net, create the sale and go to the wizard's
     Pictures step. Copy the address bar. Leave the tab open.
  2. On denveronlineauctions.com, open the auction AS A VISITOR --
     the page showing the grid of all lots -- and copy the address
     bar. It looks like:
       https://denveronlineauctions.com/auction/<auction-name>
     (NOT a sub-admin/EditAuction link -- that's one lot's edit
     form, and the agent will refuse it.)
  3. Double-click  RUN-ESTATESALES-AGENT.bat . It asks you to
     paste the two links, then:
       "Start at lot #"  -- press ENTER for a fresh sale
       "Photos to upload" -- press ENTER for ALL
  4. Chrome opens and works. Do not click inside that window.
  5. When it finishes it prints the highest lot number it did.
     Go to your estatesales.net tab, check the pictures and
     descriptions, click "Save and Continue", finish the sale.


THE RULES  (read these twice)
-----------------------------
  * ONE run per sale, into a sale with an EMPTY Pictures step.
    Descriptions are matched to pictures in order. If the sale
    already has pictures (added by hand, or from an earlier run),
    the descriptions will land on the WRONG pictures.
  * Do not run the same auction into the same sale twice -- there
    is no duplicate check on your PC. You'd get every photo twice.
    Exception: DOA added more lots later. Run again and at
    "Start at lot #" enter the number after the last lot you did.
  * Photos already up but descriptions missing? Use
    RUN-CAPTIONS-ONLY.bat  -- it skips uploading entirely.
  * Never send anyone your .estatesales-test.env or the
    .chrome-profile folder. They hold your passwords and session.
    (MAKE-DISTRIBUTION-ZIP.bat exists so David can't either.)
  * Lots with no photo on DOA yet are skipped and listed in the
    window ("No photo on DOA yet, skipped: #..."). Add the photos
    on DOA, then run again with "Start at lot #".


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
  - The black window says what happened. Read it before closing.
  - Screenshots of the moment it failed are in  screenshots\ .
  - "Password was incorrect" during a run: your saved session
    expired. Run SIGN-IN-ONCE.bat again, then re-run.
  - "Could not find ..." errors usually mean estatesales.net or
    DOA changed their page. Send David the window text and the
    newest screenshot.
  - It refused your DOA link: you pasted an admin/EditAuction
    link. Use the public auction page (see EVERY SALE step 2).


UPDATES
-------
When something changes, David sends a new zip. Unzip it over your
folder (say yes to replace), then double-click SETUP.bat once.
Your login file, Chrome profile and screenshots are kept.
